About —

I am a WordPress developer and digital agency founder from Chiniot, Pakistan. Through my agency Krenexis, I have delivered complete business websites for Pakistani and international clients, including a USA-based client — scoping, design, Elementor Pro builds, launch and support, end to end.

Alongside client work, I founded and launched two live marketplace platforms — FoneMandi (mobile phones) and Chiniot Furniture Bazar (furniture) — built with Next.js and Supabase on Cloudflare, and I run ScrollStop, an AI-driven social media agency powered by a coordinated team of AI role-agents. My goal is simple: build real, useful technology from Pakistan that businesses depend on every day. Everything listed below is live and verifiable — happy to walk through any project on a call.
